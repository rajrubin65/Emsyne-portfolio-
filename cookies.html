Cookie Policy

Transparency in how we enhance your digital experience

Last updated: March 11, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Emsyne uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. Our goal is to provide a seamless interaction while respecting your privacy.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They act as a digital memory, helping websites function properly, remember your preferences, and improve your overall user experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several critical purposes:

  • Essential Cookies – Required for the website to function properly and securely.
  • Performance Cookies – Help us understand how visitors interact with our digital environment.
  • Functional Cookies – Remember your preferences and personalized settings.
  • Analytics Cookies – Help us analyze traffic patterns to continuously improve our services.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some interactive elements may be provided by third-party services such as:

Google Analytics Embedded Videos Social Integrations

These external services may collect information according to their own privacy standards and policies.

4. Managing Cookies

You maintain full control over your digital footprint. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, please note that disabling certain cookies may impact the full functionality of our website.

5. Changes to This Policy

Technology and regulations evolve, and so does our policy. We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We encourage periodic reviews to stay informed.
